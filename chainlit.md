# 🚁 Skylark Drones — Founder Business Intelligence Agent

Welcome to your autonomous **Founder Business Intelligence (BI) Copilot**. This system connects directly to your live **monday.com Deals & Work Orders boards** to provide real-time strategic, financial, and operational clarity.

### 🌟 Key Capabilities
- **📈 Sales Pipeline & Win Rates**: Real-time pipeline valuation, deal stage conversions, sector distribution, and true win-rate calculations ($Won / (Won + Dead)$).
- **⚙️ Operations & Delivery Tracking**: Live work order execution status, billing/collected metrics, stuck invoices, and overdue risk detection.
- **🔄 Cross-Board Intelligence**: Automated joins between closed deals and work order fulfillment timelines to identify delivery bottlenecks.
- **🔍 Data Quality Audits**: Automatic anomaly detection, missing date audits, unmapped statuses, and data health scoring.
- **👔 Executive Briefings**: One-click comprehensive leadership updates structured across 5 strategic pillars.

---

### ⚡ Quick Commands
- Type **/summary** or **/leadership** for an instant 5-pillar executive update.
- Ask natural language questions like *"What is our total active pipeline value?"* or *"Which work orders are stuck in billing?"*
